# Script and Storyboard Writer

Use when the user has an idea, topic, source, or brief and needs a production-ready story plan.

Return:
- logline and audience
- verified source notes and disclosure status
- timed scene list with hook, setting, conflict, escalation, choice, resolution, and reflection
- narration per scene
- subtitle-safe on-screen text
- visual environment, characters, camera, and effects
- music and sound-design cues
- thumbnail hook and SEO handoff

Ask for missing source material before writing factual religious or historical details. Label fiction and dramatization clearly. Keep narration concise enough for the target duration and never invent quotes or citations.

For local rendering, convert the approved plan into a Video Forge template or `spec.json`, validate it, then render.
