# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, copyright, and attribution.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and publishing another person's private information are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to brokeinnovation@gmail.com. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing AgentBroko.
