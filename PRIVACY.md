# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

AgentBroko is designed to run locally. The current AgentBroko code does not require an account, collect analytics, include telemetry, upload media, or send project content to Broke Innovation servers.

## Data processed locally

Video Forge reads files you select, invokes locally installed tools such as FFmpeg or an offline speech engine, and writes outputs to paths you configure. You control those files and are responsible for protecting, backing up, and deleting them.

## External services

Installing software through npm, PyPI, GitHub, operating-system package managers, or downloading optional voices may send normal network metadata to those independent providers. Their privacy policies apply. Coding assistants or IDE extensions used with AgentBroko may also process files under their own settings and policies.

## Logs and metadata

AgentBroko prints commands and errors to the local terminal. Generated output manifests may include local file paths. Review logs and manifests before sharing them publicly.

## Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to brokeinnovation@gmail.com.
